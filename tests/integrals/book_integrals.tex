Every statement below is true. Each says that an antiderivative printed in a textbook is right, $\int f \, dx = F + c$, or that a definite integral has the printed value, $\int_{a}^{b} f \, dx = V$ (corrected in the two exercises where the printed result is wrong, as an independent check found). The engine must prove them all.
