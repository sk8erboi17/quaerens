The generation hypothesis is part of the statement. No standard natural-number
structure is assumed for the arbitrary set $S$.

\begin{enumerate}
\item $\displaystyle (z \in S \land s \colon S \to S \land f \colon S \to S \land (\forall X \subseteq S\ ((z \in X \land \forall y \in S\ (y \in X \implies s(y) \in X)) \implies \forall y \in S\ (y \in X))) \land f(z)=z \land (\forall y \in S\ (f(s(y))=s(f(y))))) \implies \forall y \in S\ (f(y)=y)$
\end{enumerate}
