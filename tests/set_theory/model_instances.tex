Every statement below is true, and each is a variant of a book statement of \texttt{equivalence\_relations.tex} or \texttt{order\_relations.tex}, not a copy. None is proved by the instances chosen by their form: the instances their proofs need are found with the candidate model of paragraph 28r. Item 1 needs an element nobody mentions, $f(a)$. Items 12 and 13 need a map that nobody writes: the law of the maps with two values (28s) gives the constant maps and those that differ from a constant at one point.
