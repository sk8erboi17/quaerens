Each item declares a function $f$, a constraint $g = 0$ and the Lagrangian $L = f + \lambda g$ with the multiplier written as a number, and states at a point that the constraint holds, that the gradient of $L$ vanishes, and the value or the sign of the determinant of the bordered Hessian matrix. The last items compare the values of $f$ at the candidates. Every statement is true.
