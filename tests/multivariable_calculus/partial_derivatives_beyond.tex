Each item is true, but asks for something the engine cannot do yet: the exact values of sine and cosine at $\frac{\pi}{3}$, $\frac{\pi}{4}$ and $\frac{\pi}{6}$, or a derivative at a point where the rules of differentiation give no formula. None may be called false. The reasons are in \texttt{partial\_derivatives\_beyond\_about.tex}.

\begin{enumerate}
\item $\displaystyle f(x, y) = \sin(x) \cos(y) \implies f_{x}\left( \frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$
\item $\displaystyle f(x, y) = \sin(x) \cos(y) \implies f_{x}\left( \frac{\pi}{3}, 0 \right) = \frac{1}{2}$
\item $\displaystyle f(x, y) = \sin(xy) \implies \nabla f\left( 1, \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \left( \frac{\pi \sqrt{2}}{8}, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$
\item $\displaystyle f(x, y) = x |x| y \implies f_{x}(0, 1) = 0$
\item $\displaystyle f(x, y) = |x|^{3} + y \implies \nabla f(0, 0) = (0, 1)$
\item $\displaystyle f(x, y) = \sqrt{x^{4} + y^{4}} \implies \nabla f(0, 0) = (0, 0)$
\end{enumerate}
