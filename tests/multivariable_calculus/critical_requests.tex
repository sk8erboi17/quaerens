Each item declares a function of two variables and asks for its critical points, the points where the gradient vanishes. The engine answers with the condition that says where they are; when the answer is a finite list of points it proves again, point by point, that the gradient vanishes there, with the sign of the determinant of the Hessian matrix. The functions come from collections of solved exercises.
