This note explains what the test data in \texttt{book\_derivatives.tex} is used for. That file is input for the engine in \texttt{main.c}; it is read by the test \texttt{test\_book\_derivatives.c} in this folder.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Purpose.} The file holds 46 true statements, $\frac{d}{dx}\left( f \right) = g$ or $\frac{d^{k}}{dx^{k}}\left( f \right) = g$: the derivative of a function is the result printed in an exercise collection (the tables of matematika.it, the solved exercises of the universities of Ferrara, Pavia, Bologna, Pisa and Trieste, and others). Where the printed result is wrong the statement has the corrected one, and the printed one is in \texttt{false\_book\_derivatives.tex}. Two statements are formulas for the derivative of order $n$.

\item \textbf{What it exercises.} The rules of differentiation of chapter 27 (product, quotient, chain; powers with the variable in the base, in the exponent or in both, read as $e^{v \ln u}$ where the base is positive; logarithms with any base, also a fraction or $e$; real cube roots; inverse trigonometric functions), their check against the Taylor series of the function (27h), and the comparison of the derivative given by the rules with the simplified form printed in the book (27a). That comparison is the hard part: the books write $\log_{\frac{2}{3}} e$, $\sin 2x$, $\frac{1}{3\sqrt[3]{(x^{3} + 2x^{2} + 1)^{2}}}$, $e^{\sin x}$, and the engine must recognise them as the same numbers by exact algebra. Two of them need the domain, where the derivative and the printed form are both defined (rules 11q and 12d): $\sqrt{1 - (\sqrt{1 - 2x})^{2}} = \sqrt{2}\sqrt{x}$ and $\sqrt{x}\sqrt{1 - 2x} = \sqrt{x(1 - 2x)}$ hold there because $x \ge 0$ and $1 - 2x \ge 0$ do; $\sqrt{1 - \sin^{2} x} = |\cos x|$, in each case of the absolute value, because the case gives the sign of $\cos x$.

\item \textbf{Variety.} 46 of the 47 derivatives of \texttt{photos/dati\_esercizi/derivate.jsonl}, in its order: the other one is in \texttt{book\_derivatives\_beyond.tex}. Each was confirmed by an independent oracle (Taylor-mode automatic differentiation in decimal arithmetic, and a difference quotient), and \texttt{tools/fuzz/fuzz\_derivatives.py} in mode \texttt{check} confirms them again.

\item \textbf{Expected result.} The line \texttt{PASS: 46 of 46 statements proved, each with a written proof}. The same statements, with the part after the equals sign removed, become requests: the line \texttt{PASS: 45 of 45 derivatives asked without their result are computed\ldots} says that every derivative asked is answered and agrees with the printed result.

\item \textbf{How to run it.} From the top folder of the repository:
\begin{verbatim}
cc -std=c11 -O2 -Wall -Wextra -o /tmp/test_book_derivatives \
    tests/derivatives/test_book_derivatives.c && /tmp/test_book_derivatives
\end{verbatim}
\end{enumerate}
