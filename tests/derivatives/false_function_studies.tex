Every statement below is false: a study of a function with one endpoint moved, an endpoint included that is not a solution, an interval that runs over a point where the function has no value, or a printed result that is wrong (the concavity of $x^{5}e^{-x}$, the period of the stationary points of $\sin 3x$). None of them may be proved.
