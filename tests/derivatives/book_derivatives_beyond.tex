The statement below is true: the printed result of an exercise collection, confirmed by an independent check. The engine does not prove it yet, because the printed form needs an identity that its exact algebra does not apply; it must never call it false.

\begin{enumerate}
\item $\displaystyle \frac{d}{dx}\left( \sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos x}{1 - \cos x}} \right) = \frac{\sin x}{|\sin x|} \cdot \frac{1}{\cos x - 1}$
\end{enumerate}
