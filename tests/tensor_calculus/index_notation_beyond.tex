Statements with a Kronecker delta or a Levi-Civita symbol that the reader does not accept: the dimension is not declared, the summation convention is broken, or the notation belongs to a later stage (derivatives, vectors, powers, a metric that is not diagonal), or a hypothesis leaves out what it has to declare (a metric, the positions of the indices of a table, the values of Greek indices). Some of them are true in their intended reading; none may be proved or refuted.
