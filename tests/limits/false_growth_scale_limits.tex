Every statement below is false. They are the limits of \texttt{growth\_scale\_limits.tex} with a wrong value: the infinity with the other sign, $0$ for an infinite limit and the converse, a finite value changed, and ``does not exist'' said of a limit that exists. The engine must never prove them.
